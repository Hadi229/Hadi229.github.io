Featured: FS5 Load Slammer

● IN PROGRESS · REV 3 SCHEMATIC

A 50 A transient-load tester for Formula Slug's LV power supplies

A load slammer suddenly yanks a large, fast current step out of a power supply so you can watch on a scope how well it holds regulation. I'm designing ours from the ground up in KiCad for the FS5 car's custom LV supplies.

~50 A
Peak load step
5 mΩ
Kelvin current sense
BNC
Trigger in / I-mon out
  • Closed-loop linear control: moved from a hard-switched gate driver (UCC27511) to an op-amp loop (OPA994) that drives the MOSFET in its linear region — current tracks the trigger waveform, so slew rate and step size are adjustable.
  • Power stage: IPB017N10N5LF 100 V / 1.7 mΩ MOSFET with a single 200 mΩ high-power load resistor (replaced a 12-resistor parallel bank to cut footprint).
  • Measurement: 4-terminal 5 mΩ sense resistor with a net-tie separating power and signal ground; 50 Ω-terminated coax trigger and current-monitor output for clean scope captures.
  • Gate network: series gate resistor to damp ringing, 10 kΩ pull-down so the FET fails safe-off.

Rev 2: refactored to the FS-LV standards with labeled subsystems — Logic Regulator, Gate Control, Load Bank, Switch & Sense.
